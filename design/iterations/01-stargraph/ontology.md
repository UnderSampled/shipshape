# Stargraph ontology:
  - environments
    - gravitational bodies (planets, e.g.)
    - asteroid fields
    - dead space
  - stations
  - ships
  - drones
  - modules (attach to hardpoints on ships, stations, and drones, limited by their size)
  - items
    - non-fungible quest items
    - rare artifacts
    - fungible trade-goods and consumable resources

More types and details can be added as needed, these are just the major ones.
Note how these are listed generally by size. Items might be stored in modules, and a station might be in an asteroid field, but it's unlikey for a ship to be in a drone. A drone might be in a ship's module, but only a very small drone might be in another drone's module.
